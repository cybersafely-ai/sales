## Product (use approved claims only — see `approved-claims.md`)

- A **parental safety platform** built around a **smart keyboard** on the child's device. Risk signals (cyberbullying, sextortion, drug references, self-harm) trigger **instant parent alerts** with severity and context. Passwords are never collected; it "doesn't retain a record of the child's day."
- Contract framing: **"behavioral intelligence: pattern detection over time, not a keyword scanner, not a content filter."**
- ⚠️ Claim tension: the Partner Guide says it "checks typed content in real time" while the contract says "not a keyword scanner." Both may be true — do not use either in public/parent copy until the approved-claims list is confirmed.
- **Parents subscribe and parents do the monitoring.** It is not school-operated monitoring.

## Business model (pricing — do not exceed the Partner Guide)

- **$150/year per student.**
- **20% of every subscription goes to the school**, every year the family stays — pitched as a **fundraiser** that also gives families a child-safety tool.
- Distribution historically runs on **referral codes and QR codes** from the internal **staff portal** (which also holds analytics). HubSpot is the CRM. A directory of **2,444 Missouri schools** exists.
- The affiliate program adds a **second route to families** (teachers + creators) alongside principal-led outreach.

## The activation funnel (what "activated" means)

land with code → register + billing → add child → install app on child's device → **enable keyboard** → first alert configured → ongoing use.

**Activation = child added and keyboard enabled.** This is the step that makes an affiliate reward payable. The keyboard-enable step is the most likely drop-off point.
